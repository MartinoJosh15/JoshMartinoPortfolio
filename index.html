Approach

I want to understand both the model and the system around it.

My AI work is grounded in implementation. I have built gradient propagation myself, reconstructed the components of GPT-2, optimized a modern training loop, and connected AI to live robotics data. That systems background helps me think beyond a notebook toward reliable, useful AI products.

How I Work

  • I learn core ML concepts deeply enough to implement them, not just call them.
  • I treat data flow, training stability, evaluation, and deployment as one system.
  • I connect AI models to real applications, from robotics telemetry to product workflows.
  • I bring backend, cloud, and full-stack experience to machine learning work.
