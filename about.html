How I got here

From model internals to physical systems

I got interested in AI because I wanted to understand what the libraries were doing for me. That led from implementing reverse-mode automatic differentiation to rebuilding GPT-2 in PyTorch and working on a CUDA-accelerated training pipeline.

JACart pushed that interest into a physical system. I have worked across ROS 2, LiDAR, cameras, localization, Docker networking, and multimodal inference. Debugging a real vehicle means checking timing, transforms, services, and hardware together instead of treating the model as an isolated component.

I enjoy projects where software, AI, and systems overlap, especially when there is a concrete engineering problem to trace and solve. Teaching Python and helping lead technical communities at JMU have also made clear communication a regular part of how I work.
