Engineering philosophy

Models, infrastructure, and real-world behavior belong in the same conversation.

01 / FIRST PRINCIPLES

Learn by building the mechanism

Reimplementing GPT-2 and reverse-mode automatic differentiation made the math and framework abstractions concrete. I use that depth to debug with a better mental model.

02 / COMPLETE SYSTEMS

Care about what surrounds the model

Training code, sensor timing, queues, APIs, persistence, observability, and user workflows determine whether an intelligent feature becomes a dependable system.

03 / PHYSICAL CONTEXT

Connect ML to the real world

Autonomous-vehicle research turns abstract model work into integration constraints: incomplete sensor data, localization health, networked services, and operators who need useful evidence.

04 / EVIDENCE

Debug, measure, iterate

I prefer a traceable experiment and an honest limitation over a vague claim. Reliability comes from observing the system, isolating variables, and improving it deliberately.
